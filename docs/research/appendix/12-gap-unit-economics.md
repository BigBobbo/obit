## 1. MuchLoved (UK) — the only audited player: real, but it took ~15 years
**Verified (regulator data).** Muchloved Charitable Trust (CCEW 1118590, reg. 28 Mar 2007) income by year (Charity Commission data via findthatcharity.uk): 2007 £6.6k; 2013 £212k; 2019 £518k; 2020 £824k; 2021 £1.26M; 2022 £1.25M; 2023 £1.90M (spend £1.71M); then a collapse to £51k (2024) and £131k (2025). The collapse is explained by a spin-out: **MuchLoved Limited** (Companies House 14965211) was incorporated 27 Jun 2023; founder Jonathan Walter Davies was 75%+ controller until 31 Dec 2023, when the Charitable Trust became the PSC with a 25–50% stake — i.e., the tribute operation now runs as a company part-owned by the charity. MuchLoved Limited's FY2025 accounts (unaudited small-company, filed 27 Jul 2026): **average 27 employees (2024: 21)**; net assets £624,286 (2024: £406,376); retained P&L reserve £430,786 vs £212,876 → **≥£218k retained post-tax profit in FY2025**; cash £389k; no P&L filed (small-companies exemption). Note: charity income excluded pass-through donations MuchLoved processes for other charities. Interpretation: a genuine, profitable ~27-person business — but it took ~15 years to cross £1M income, got a COVID demand shock (2019→2021 income +144%), and its engine is funeral-director/charity distribution, not family-direct subscriptions.

## 2. ForeverMissed — 17-year family-direct incumbent, lifestyle-business profile
**Verified (first-party).** Founded "end of 2008" by **Oleg Andelman** (about page, fetched Aug 2026). Homepage currently claims **"310,000+ families across 47 countries."** Pricing: free tier; Premium $9.95/mo or $79.95/yr; **Lifetime $159.95**. No headcount could be verified (LinkedIn/Growjo/ZoomInfo blocked); Bing SERP metadata showed ~1K Trustpilot reviews and a Facebook page with ~196.7k likes — consistent with prior research. Back-of-envelope (estimate, low confidence): 310k families over 17 years ≈ 18k memorials/yr; even at a generous 15% ever-paid × ~$100 blended, that is low-single-digit-$M cumulative revenue — a solo/small-team lifestyle business, not a scale outcome. It has, however, sustained its founder for 17+ years.

## 3. Keeper Memorials — 30+ people claimed, B2B-led; VA link NOT verified
**Verified (first-party, mykeeper.com).** Founded **2013 in Montreal as Qeepr**; founder Mandy Benoualid. About page: "In 2023 the Keeper Memorial team has expanded to **30+ team members across 20 cities** and is servicing **over 5 million memorial pages in 238+ countries**" — self-reported; "238+ countries" exceeds the number of countries on Earth, so treat the 5M figure as marketing (likely including B2B-imported obituary records). Pricing: free (5 photos); **Keeper Plus $99 one-time**; **Concierge $350 one-time**; QR plaque shop; white-label B2B for funeral homes, cemeteries, hospice; cemetery kiosks. Named B2B clients: Holocaust Memorial Day Trust (bespoke branded build) and Mount Sinai Memorial Parks and Mortuaries. **Funding: unverifiable** (Crunchbase/PitchBook inaccessible; no BetaKit coverage exists for "Qeepr" — a notable absence for a Montreal startup, weakly suggesting little/no institutional money). **VA Veterans Legacy Memorial claim: could not be verified.** VLM (vlm.cem.va.gov) is VA/NCA-operated; its site names no vendor, and Keeper's own site/llms-full.txt makes no VLM or veterans-program claim. Treat the reported relationship as unconfirmed.

## Synthesis for the go/no-go
The best evidence says the niche sustains **small profitable businesses, slowly**: the one audited player (MuchLoved) needed ~15 years and funeral-trade B2B distribution to reach ~£1–2M income and now runs 27 staff at ~£200k/yr profit; the 17-year family-direct incumbent (ForeverMissed) claims 310k families and appears founder-scale; the 13-year player (Keeper) claims 30+ people and leans on B2B white-label plus one-time $99/$350 SKUs and QR hardware. No family-direct memorial company shows any verified 7-figure-profit, consumer-subscription outcome. The pattern that does monetize: **one-time payments (Keeper $99/$350, ForeverMissed $159.95 lifetime, Turning Hearts ~$99 hardware) and funeral-home/cemetery distribution** — both compatible with this product's QR-plaque, near-zero-ops positioning; none of it validates building a social network.
